How to fix "This template is too large to save" in Outlook

June 10, 2026

You write a template in Outlook's My Templates panel, click save, and get: "This template is too large to save. Please make it smaller, then try to save it again." The template might be two paragraphs long. What gives?

The real reason: a 32 KB cap on everything

My Templates - the built-in template add-in in Outlook on the web and new Outlook - stores all of your templates in a single hidden mailbox property. That property has a hard limit of 32 KB total. Not per template - for all templates combined.

Plain text is small, so a handful of short replies fit fine. But formatting markup counts against the limit too, and a pasted image blows past it instantly. Once you approach the cap, every new template triggers the error, even a short one.

What you can do inside Outlook

  • Trim formatting. Paste templates as plain text. Formatted text carries hidden markup that eats the budget.
  • Remove images. Even a small logo is usually tens of kilobytes - more than the entire allowance.
  • Delete old templates. The cap is shared, so removing unused templates frees space for new ones.
  • Use Quick Parts (classic desktop Outlook only). The old desktop client has a roomier building-blocks system, but it does not exist in Outlook on the web.

These help, but they all amount to the same thing: squeezing your work into 32 KB. If you write more than a few templated replies a day, you will hit the wall again.

The way around the limit entirely

Browser extensions store templates in the browser itself, not in the mailbox property - so the 32 KB cap simply does not apply.
