SnipMail Pro is on the way

SnipMail is free to use right now. A Pro upgrade is planned for power users - one time, no subscription, no account, and the license will work offline like the rest of the extension.

Pro - planned, not on sale yet

Want Pro when it lands? Email me and I'll let you know - no commitment.

What Pro will add

  • Unlimited snippetsThe free plan covers 5 snippets. Pro will remove the limit entirely.
  • Placeholders{date} and {time} fill in automatically when you insert a snippet. {cursor} puts the caret exactly where you want to keep typing.
  • Import & exportBack up your snippets to a JSON file and move them to another computer in two clicks.

Folders and per-snippet hotkeys are on the roadmap too. Everything the core extension does today stays free.

How it will work

When Pro is ready, the plan is a single one-time upgrade - no recurring fee, nothing to cancel. The license will be verified inside the extension itself, so it keeps working offline and doesn't depend on any server staying online. Your snippets stay in your browser either way.

Questions you'd probably ask

Is Pro available yet? Not yet. The core extension is free and that's the focus today. Pro lands once the free version has proven itself useful to enough people.

Will it be a subscription? No. The plan is a one-time upgrade. If recurring billing ever shows up, it won't be SnipMail.

What if Outlook, Gmail or another mail service changes after an update? Web mail clients change their interfaces now and then; fixing that is my job, and updates ship through the browser store automatically - for free and Pro alike.
