Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

The short version

SnipMail collects nothing. Your snippets never leave your browser.

What data SnipMail stores

The snippets you create (shortcut and text) and, if you buy Pro, your license key are stored using Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device. That is the only data the extension stores, and it stays on your computer. The license key is verified locally - no activation server is ever contacted.

What data SnipMail collects or transmits

None. The extension:

  • makes no network requests of any kind;
  • has no analytics, telemetry or tracking;
  • does not read, store or transmit the content of your emails - it only inserts text where you ask it to;
  • requires no account or sign-up.

Permissions explained

  • storage - used to save your snippets locally in your browser.
  • Access to outlook.live.com / outlook.office.com / outlook.office365.com / mail.google.com - the extension runs only on Outlook Web and Gmail pages so it can show the snippet picker and insert text into the email you are writing.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the extension removes all stored snippets. You can also delete any snippet at any time from the extension popup.

Changes to this policy

If the privacy model ever changes (for example, an optional sync feature), this page will be updated before the change ships, and the extension listing will reflect it.
