SnipMail inserts your saved templates into Outlook Web and Gmail the moment you type /. No account, no cloud, no subscription - your snippets stay in your browser.

Launching on the Chrome Web Store in a few days

Free to install. Works on outlook.live.com, outlook.office.com and mail.google.com

How it works

  1. Save a snippet in the SnipMail popup - a shortcut and the text. For example /intro for your usual introduction.
  2. Write an email in Outlook Web or Gmail and type /intro right in the message body.
  3. Pick the snippet from the dropdown (or just hit Enter) - the text is inserted where your cursor is. Done.

Honest limitations

SnipMail works in the browser: Outlook Web (outlook.live.com, outlook.office.com) and Gmail (mail.google.com). It does not work in the Outlook or Gmail desktop/mobile apps. Some companies restrict browser extensions on managed devices - if your IT department locks Chrome down, check with them first.
